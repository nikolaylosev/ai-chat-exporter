Save your AI conversations to your own computer, with every file in them. Claude, Gemini, Grok and ChatGPT chats become Markdown notes, and the files of each chat are saved next to the note in their original format.

WHY THIS ONE
• Every file, not only the text: your uploads, documents and code the model wrote, Claude artifacts, ChatGPT canvases and generated images, saved as they are.
• Projects of Claude, ChatGPT and Grok with their instructions, files and chats, and Gemini notebooks with their chats.
• Grok as well as Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT.
• Hundreds of chats at once, in the background, skipping the ones already saved.
• No account, no server, no tracking. Free and open source.

WHY KEEP A COPY
• Chats can be deleted, lost with an account, or hidden behind a plan change.
• Search and reuse them where you keep your notes, with their files, instead of scrolling through each service.
• Move your history between services, or back it up before you clean up old chats.

WHAT A NOTE HOLDS
• The whole conversation as Markdown with YAML properties (title, dates, model, project), ready for an Obsidian vault or any notes app.
• Thinking, web searches and tool calls as collapsible blocks, and other branches of a chat if you want them.
• Or the same note as an HTML page that opens in any browser, a Word document with the chat's images inside, or JSON.

HOW YOU USE IT
• The chat you are reading: click the icon and "Save this chat", or press Alt+Shift+S (Option+Shift+S on a Mac). "Copy" puts the chat on the clipboard as Markdown instead.
• Only some messages: "Choose messages…" lists the chat's messages; tick the ones you want (or only yours, or only the AI's replies) and save or copy just them.
• Many chats or projects: open the export page, filter by date, project or title, tick the ones you want or all of them, and save. It runs in the background, can be cancelled, and writes a report of anything that failed.
• Into Downloads, a chat with its files comes as one .zip, and its text alone as one file. Or straight into a folder you choose, file by file.

PRIVACY
• Everything stays on your computer. The extension has no server and sends your conversations nowhere.
• It reads your chats through your existing session on each service, the same way the service's own web page does. It never stores your passwords or access tokens.
• Open source (MIT): https://github.com/nikolaylosev/ai-chat-exporter

SUPPORTED SERVICES
claude.ai, gemini.google.com, grok.com, chatgpt.com. Interface in English and Russian.
